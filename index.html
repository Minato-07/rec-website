ABOUT REC

More Than Just a Gaming Club

RUET Esports Community (REC) brings together students who are passionate about competitive gaming, teamwork, leadership and technology. Our goal is to build a friendly and competitive environment where every gamer can improve, compete and proudly represent RUET.

Why Join REC?

Whether you're a casual player or an experienced competitor, REC provides a platform to meet teammates, participate in tournaments, attend gaming events and improve your competitive skills.

We believe esports is more than playing games—it's about communication, collaboration, discipline and building a strong community.
